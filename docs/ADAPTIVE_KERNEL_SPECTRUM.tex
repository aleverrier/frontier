\paragraph{Status.}
Rigorous finite-model majorant and deterministic distance-three surface-code
calculation.  This completes the preceding boundary-shift investigation.
Retaining compatibility, aggregation, shift-specific score loads, and the
exact trimmed head gives
\begin{align*}
 \sum_tD_t^{\rm cap}&\le0.2519

These are bounds on syndrome-averaged exact posterior mass first removed by

\section{Low-weight compatibility}

At a fixed cut, a live singleton has empty completed-check signature.  A
live pair consists of two columns with the same nonempty completed-check
signature.  Thus singletons are mutually compatible, at most one pair can be
chosen from each signature class, and two classes are compatible exactly
when their signatures are disjoint.

Dominant surface cuts contain 392--563 live size-one/two polymers but only
20--33 pair-signature classes.  Some classes contain 120--378 nominal pair
choices, of which at most one is legal.  An exact row-mask set-packing
dynamic program and Walsh transform enforce both compatibility and boundary
XOR aggregation.  This lowers the optimized partial threshold to about 1509
for X and 1455 for Z, but the \(K=1024\) partial right-hand sides remain
1.819 and 1.750.

\section{Shift-specific Finner order}

For active-detector shift \(\delta\), define
\[
 \lambda_t(\delta)=
 \max_{j\in J_t}
 \sum_{\substack{i\in\Gamma_t\\\delta_i=1,\ H_{ij}=1}}\alpha_i.
\]
The Finner condition is pointwise in the relative shift, so choose
\[
 \theta_t^\star(\delta)=
 \min\left\{\frac12,\frac1{\lambda_t(\delta)}\right\},
 \qquad \theta_t^\star(0)=\frac12.
\]
For scalar \(\alpha=0.8\), the retained surface model uses only
\[
 \theta^\star\in\left\{\frac5{16},\frac5{12},\frac12\right\}.
\]
This changes the size-one/two grouped fractional right-hand side at
\(K=1024\) from 1.819 to 0.254 for X and from 1.750 to 0.236 for Z.

\section{All-size kernel majorant}

Put
\[
 a_{\theta,j}=
 (1-p_j)^{1-\theta}p_j^\theta+
 p_j^{1-\theta}(1-p_j)^\theta
\]
and define
\[
 \mathcal Z_{t,\theta}(\xi)=
 \sum_{\substack{
 v\in\F_2^{I_t}\\
 H_{C_t,I_t}v=0\\
 D_tv=\xi}}
 \prod_{j:v_j=1}a_{\theta,j}.
\]

\paragraph{Visible-family injection.}
Every compatible family \(\mathcal F\) of visible open-prefix polymers maps
injectively to its union vector
\[
 v_{\mathcal F}=\bigoplus_{\gamma\in\mathcal F}\1_\gamma.
\]
The completed-check connected components recover the family from the union,
\(H_{C_t,I_t}v_{\mathcal F}=0\), and
\(D_tv_{\mathcal F}=D_t\mathcal F\).  Compatibility makes the family weight
equal to the product over the union.  Hence the compatible visible-family
partition is bounded pointwise by \(\mathcal Z_{t,\theta}\).
The latter may additionally count vectors containing invisible components.

Combining this injection with the bounded-hypergraph pointwise theorem gives
\[
 \omega_t(\xi)\le z_t(\xi):=
 \mathcal Z_{t,\theta_t^\star(\delta_t\xi)}(\xi).
\]

\section{Exact active-frontier recurrence}

For each fixed \(\theta\), maintain a weighted state on the active detector
residual \(r\) and logical prefix \(\ell\).  Before row closure at column
\(j\), update
\[
 P_j(r,\ell)\leftarrow
 P_{j-1}(r,\ell)+
 a_{\theta,j}P_{j-1}(r+H_j,\ell+L_j).
\]
Discard states with nonzero residual on a closing row, remove the closed
coordinates, and record the remaining boundary spectrum.  This exactly
enumerates every vector in \(\mathcal Z_{t,\theta}\).

The retained surface models have at most eleven active detectors and one
logical bit, so each of the three fixed-\(\theta\) recurrences has at most
4096 states.

\section{Exact trimmed head}

Let \(z_{t,(1)}\ge z_{t,(2)}\ge\cdots\) be the ordered nonzero-shift
majorant.  Pointwise monotonicity in the recursive cap theorem gives
\[
 \boxed{
 D_t^{\rm cap}\le
 \inf_{0\le q<K}
 \frac{\sum_{j>q}z_{t,(j)}}{K-q}.}
\]
This also captures the finite-support endpoint: fewer than \(K\) nonzero
relative shifts cannot supply \(K\) outrankers.

The best sampled fractional corollary for the same all-size spectrum is
still vacuous at \(K=1024\): 1.927 for X and 2.068 for Z.  The exact trimmed
calculation gives 0.2519 and 0.2767.

\section{Interpretation}

The finite surface certificate needs four mechanisms:
\begin{enumerate}
\item compatibility of connected components;
\item aggregation by boundary XOR shift;
\item shift-specific Finner load;
\item exact dominant-head trimming and finite support.
\end{enumerate}
Worst-case width controls the cost of an exact proof computation but does
not determine cap-induced error.  The relevant object is the ordered,
score-calibrated comparison spectrum.

The result does not yet give a scalable BB144/Gross certificate: a split
sector has up to 134 active detectors.  It also does not address score-gap
loss.

\section{Terminal ranking is a separate question}

The public Gross \(p=0.001,\Delta=20,K=16384\) summary has three logical
failures in \(10^8\) trials, zero truth-missing cases, and three
truth-present-but-not-selected cases.  In the replay code, this means the
sampled logical sector retained nonzero mass but another retained sector had
larger mass.

That classification excludes empty logical support, but it does not prove
that pruning caused the wrong ranking.  Exact logical ML also fails when the
sampled true sector is not posterior-maximal.  Separating algorithmic
distortion from intrinsic Bayes error requires an exact logical-ML
comparator or exact sector survival fractions
\[
 a_\ell(s)=\frac{\widehat Z_\ell(s)}{Z_\ell(s)}.
\]
If \(\ell_*\) is the exact logical-ML sector, Frontier agrees whenever
\[
 \max_{\ell\ne\ell_*}\log\frac{a_\ell}{a_{\ell_*}}
 <
 \min_{\ell\ne\ell_*}\log\frac{Z_{\ell_*}}{Z_\ell}.
\]
